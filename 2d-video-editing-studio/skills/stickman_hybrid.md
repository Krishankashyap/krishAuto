# Stickman Storytelling Explainer Style

## Visual Language
- **Character Anatomy**: Clean white circular head, bold black ink outlines (3–5px), expressive eyebrows, dynamic mouth states (smirk, shock, laughter).
- **Wardrobe**: Oversized hoodies with drawstrings (orange `#FF6B35`, cobalt blue `#1D3557`), rolled denim shorts, white sneakers, backward cap.
- **Scenic Staging & Selective Color**: 2D hand-drawn scenic environments (canyons, turbulent river rapids, cliff edges, warm study desks) in muted or slate tones, with the stick figure and hero props popping in vibrant saturated color.
- **Directorial Rule**: Strictly zero photorealistic live-action photos or 3D CGI renders.

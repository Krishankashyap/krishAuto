# Kinetic Typography & Title Sequences

## Directorial Rules
- **Anti-Clutter Law**: Maximum 2 text elements per scene (never stack kicker + title + badge + callout + subtitle simultaneously).
- **Editorial Kickers**: Rendered at 46px+ at 1080p in 5 rotating designs (`bar`, `chip`, `bracket`, `rule`, `index`).
- **Inline Text Markup**: Support for `*accent*`, `_highlight_`, `[box]`, `~underline~`, and `^alt^` formatting.

# Vox Documentary Explainer Style

## Visual Language
- **Canvas Texture**: Tactile off-white paper (`#F4ECE1`), halftone dot printing patterns, subtle grid paper.
- **Color Palette**: Burnt Orange (`#E65100`), Crimson (`#D32F2F`), Navy (`#1D3557`), Deep Slate (`#111111`).
- **Signature Recipes**:
  1. `stat_hero`: Giant Anton numerals (`46`, `2430m`) flanked by category pill labels and guideline calipers.
  2. `polaroid_pin`: Archival slides and Polaroids fastened by 3D colorful pushpins on antique maps.
  3. `arch_orb`: High-contrast black & white halftone architecture over giant circular background orbs.
  4. `seesaw_compare`: Tilted seesaw comparing two opposing entities, economies, or metrics.
  5. `light_cone`: Halftone dot-pattern projection cone expanding from a retro TV across the subject.

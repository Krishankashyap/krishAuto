# Suspense Noir / Crime Documentary Style

## Visual Language
- **Full-Bleed Cinematics**: 70%+ full-bleed photographic chiaroscuro scenes with deep shadows and rim lighting.
- **Forensic Badges**: Monospaced CCTV timestamp tags with flashing red recording dots, black redaction censor bars (`redact_bar`), and pinned evidence Polaroids connected by red threads.
- **Motif Budget**: Maximum 2 rubber stamps per video (e.g. `CLASSIFIED`), separated by at least 3 scenes.

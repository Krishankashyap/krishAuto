# Pop-Art Cutouts & Sticker Hybrid Style

## Visual Language
- **Crisp White Vinyl Die-Cut Contours**: Every prop and character receives an uninterrupted 4–8px solid white outer border (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Two-Tier Warm Ambient Drop Shadows**: Soft contact shadow (0.16 alpha) + ambient diffused shadow (0.12 alpha), warm-tinted and feathered to 0.0 before canvas edges.
- **Satirical Metaphors**: Parody branding (Adobe cigarette packs, software pens), detailed anatomical illustrations with torn paper edges, comic speech balloons (`"WHAT?!"`), and extruded 3D comic badges (`"GOOD!"`).

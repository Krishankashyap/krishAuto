# 1. 🖊️ STICKMAN STYLE: The Modern Storytelling & Explainer Archetype

### 🚫 What Stickman is NOT:
- **NOT** a primitive 1-pixel line scribble on pure blank white canvas.
- **NOT** an uncanny multi-pose cartoon puppet with glitchy mouth flapping.
- **NOT** disconnected stick figures floating without an environment.

### ✅ What Stickman ACTUALLY IS (The Reference Standard):
Modern YouTube explainers (in the lineage of Casually Explained, Alan Becker, MinutePhysics, and modern animated essayists) combine **bold expressive 2D stick characters** with **rich narrative environments, selective color contrast, and cinematic lighting**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Muted Monochrome / Greyscale Landscape                │
│  (Mountains, Canyon, Turbulent Rapids, Cross-Hatching) │
│                                                        │
│             ┌─────────┐                                │
│             │ (● _ ●) │  ← Expressive White Circle Head │
│             └────┬────┘    (Smirk, Shock 'O', Grimace) │
│               ┌──┴──┐                                  │
│     Stick ───►│ 👕  │◄── Bold Colored Casual Hoodie/Tee │
│     Limbs     └──┬──┘    (Hands in Pockets / Satchel)  │
│                 / \                                    │
│                👟 👟   ← Detailed Shoes / Sneakers     │
│                                                        │
│   ⭐ Selective Color Hero: Character & Prop Pop Vibrant │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1.1 Character Anatomy & Wardrobe
1. **Head & Facial Rig**:
   - Perfect white circular head with thick clean black ink outline (3px-5px).
   - **Eyes**: Bold solid black dots, pill ovals, or wide shock circles with concentric alarm rings.
   - **Eyebrows**: Highly expressive curved arches (raised skepticism, downward determination, quizzical single-brow cock).
   - **Mouth**: Clean line smirk, broad toothy crescent grin, or gasping open `O` with tongue visible.
   - **Hair (Optional)**: 3 spiky ink tufts on top, or stylized dark brown/black anime fringe.
2. **Clothing & Props (The "Hybrid" Secret)**:
   - Stickman characters wear **modern casual or thematic outfits** with soft 2D cel shading:
     - Oversized hoodie (light grey, charcoal, black) with drawstrings and kangaroo pouch.
     - Rolled-up blue denim shorts with hands tucked in pockets.
     - Explorer fedora hat + brown leather crossbody satchel bag + orange t-shirt.
     - Backward black baseball cap with snapback holes.
     - Footwear: White sneakers with black rubber soles and laces, or simple rounded black loafers.
3. **Limbs & Gestures**:
   - Simple, confident black stick lines for arms and legs.
   - Hands: Simple 3-finger mitten hands, hands in pockets, or holding detailed props (maps, books, magnifying glass, oars).

### 1.2 Staging, Composition & Lighting
- **Selective Color Contrast**: The background environment is rendered in muted monochrome or slate greyscale (canyons, rapids, rocks, fork in the road), while the stickman and hero prop (orange shirt, yellow inflatable raft, brown map) are rendered in rich, saturated color.
- **Atmospheric & Cinematic Lighting**:
  - Golden hour window light pouring onto a wooden desk, casting long warm shadows across notebooks, pens, and the stickman's face.
  - Night cone of warm yellow street light illuminating a tired stickman leaning against a curved street lamp post.
- **Narrative Visual Metaphors**:
  - Standing at a fork in the road holding an unfolded paper map looking puzzled, with a wooden directional signpost pointing left and right.
  - Studying intensely at a desk covered in notebooks and scattered paper sheets.
  - Braving turbulent white-water rapids in a yellow inflatable boat with an oar.

### 1.3 Master Prompt Formula for Google Flow (Stickman)
```text
A modern 2D animation storytelling illustration of [CHARACTER_ACTION], [OUTFIT_DESCRIPTION, e.g. wearing an oversized grey hoodie with drawstrings and dark stick legs], clean white circle head with expressive [EMOTION, e.g. curious raised eyebrow and smirk], thick black ink contour outlines, 2D vector cel shading. [BACKGROUND_DESCRIPTION, e.g. muted monochrome slate grey canyon with a fork in the road and wooden signpost], selective color contrast with the character popping vibrantly, clean paper texture, Alan Becker and Casually Explained editorial style, 9:16 vertical, masterpiece, crisp lines.
```

# 2. 🗞️ VOX DOCUMENTARY STYLE: High-End Editorial Collage & Motion Graphics

### 🚫 What Vox Style is NOT:
- **NOT** a plain stock photograph with a random sticker pasted over it.
- **NOT** chaotic, vibrating typography and jittering elements.
- **NOT** arbitrary generic icons scattered without narrative meaning.

### ✅ What Vox Style ACTUALLY IS (The Reference Standard):
Vox, Johnny Harris, MagnatesMedia, and Polymatter use **tactile paper collage, Swiss graphic design, archival engravings, selective monochrome isolation, and conceptual data metaphors**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Warm Tactile Paper Canvas (#F4ECE1) / Grid Pattern    │
│                                                        │
│   [ 1966 ]  ← Minimal Monospaced Date Chip             │
│                                                        │
│     █████████   ← Halftone Architecture Cutout         │
│    ██ CIA HQ ██   (High Contrast B&W)                  │
│     █████████                                          │
│         ▲                                              │
│         │ Dotted Isometric Trajectory Line             │
│         ▼                                              │
│      ┌─────┐       ┌────────────────────────────────┐  │
│      │ 46  │  ───► │ $100 Bill Money Sacks Overflow │  │
│      └─────┘       └────────────────────────────────┘  │
│    Giant Stat            Physical Cutout Prop          │
│                                                        │
│  🟡 Bold Background Color Orb (Burnt Orange / Red)    │
│  ✏️ Translucent Yellow Highlighter Sweep on Keywords   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 The 6 Core Vox Composition Recipes
1. **The Giant Stat Hero (`stat_hero`)**:
   - Huge bold statistic (e.g. `46`, `2430m`, `3,1`) in deep navy or black heavyweight serif/sans.
   - Category or metric label in a dark ribbon pill or translucent marker banner (`above sea level`, `billion dollars`).
   - Isometric perspective guideline (dotted red or white line) leading to the hero cutout.
2. **The Architectural Collage & Giant Color Orb (`arch_orb`)**:
   - Architectural landmark (Leaning Tower of Pisa, Colosseum, CIA headquarters, Toronto CN Tower skyline) in high-contrast black & white halftone.
   - Layered in front of a **giant solid circular color orb** (burnt orange `#E65100`, crimson red `#D32F2F`, or cyan blue).
   - Surrealist tactile framing: e.g. A vintage engraving etched giant open palm holding up the entire city or landmark!
3. **The Pinned Archival Document & Polaroid (`polaroid_pin`)**:
   - Pinned Polaroid photo with vintage border and handwritten/monospaced caption.
   - Pinned with a 3D colorful pushpin (lime green `#76FF03`, vivid red `#FF1744`) casting a crisp contact shadow.
   - Antique archival map background with national borders outlined in neon green or orange marker.
4. **The Conceptual Visual Metaphor (`visual_metaphor`)**:
   - *"The GDP Is High"*: A tiny businessman with briefcase tightrope-walking on a thin wire stretched between two jagged 100-dollar bill mountain peaks!
   - *Rural vs Urban Seesaw*: A blue beam on a pink triangle fulcrum tilting under the weight of modern skyscrapers vs a rural wooden cabin.
   - *AI & Energy Grid*: 3D server racks with snaking power cables connecting to GPU chips, high-voltage electric pylons, and cooling towers over a red banner.
5. **The Editorial Light Cone / Projector Ray (`light_cone`)**:
   - A retro television set, film projector, or spotlight on one side.
   - A bold, expanding halftone dot-pattern cone of bright yellow/amber light (`#FFB300`) sweeping across the canvas to illuminate the subject.
   - Minimalist Swiss typography running angled along the projection border: `TELEVISION - DRUG OF THE NATION`.
6. **Selective Monochrome Isolation (`selective_mono`)**:
   - A full row of brownstone townhouses or storefronts in black & white—except for ONE single building in the center in vibrant brick red, green door, and warm brown roof.
   - Headline in clean Helvetica: `"As a small, growing business"`. The visual directly mirrors the spoken concept!

### 2.2 Color Palette & Textures
- **Background Canvases**:
  - `Warm Paper`: `#F4ECE1` to `#EDE6D8` with faint fiber noise.
  - `Graph/Grid Paper`: Off-white paper with 32px subtle green or grey isometric grid lines.
  - `Cutting Mat`: Deep forest green `#1B3B2B` with white cutting grid lines.
  - `Dark Investigation`: Charcoal slate `#12151C` with subtle white coordinate grid (+).
- **Accent Highlighters**:
  - Fluorescent Yellow: `#FFE600` (translucent marker sweep).
  - Safety Orange: `#FF6D00` (burnt orange circular background orbs).
  - Terminal Green: `#00E676` (forensic map borders and pushpins).
  - Censor Red: `#E53935` (trendline arrows and comparison fulcrums).

### 2.3 Master Prompt Formula for Google Flow (Vox Style)
```text
High-end editorial documentary collage illustration of [SUBJECT, e.g. vintage CIA headquarters architecture in high-contrast black and white halftone print], layered with [PROPS, e.g. sacks overflowing with 100-dollar bills and a floating official seal badge], set against [BACKGROUND, e.g. a warm cream tactile paper canvas #F4ECE1 with subtle isometric graph paper grid], accompanied by [GRAPHIC_MOTIF, e.g. a bold burnt-orange circular sun orb behind the building and dotted red perspective trajectory lines], Swiss modernist graphic design, Johnny Harris and Vox Borders editorial collage aesthetic, 16:9 widescreen or 9:16 vertical, ultra sharp, archival museum print quality.
```

# 3. 🏷️ STICKER STYLE: Pop-Art Cutouts & Die-Cut Badges

### 🚫 What Sticker Style is NOT:
- **NOT** a transparent image without borders that blends awkwardly into the background.
- **NOT** a flat square crop.

### ✅ What Sticker Style ACTUALLY IS (The Reference Standard):
- **Crisp Die-Cut White Vinyl Border**: Every character, satirical pop-art prop, and text badge is enclosed in a solid, thick white contour border (4px-8px width, 100% opacity) with smooth rounded corners.
- **Two-Tier Contact Shadow**:
  - Contact Shadow: Soft black (0.16 alpha, 4px blur, 2px down).
  - Ambient Shadow: Warm soft spread (0.12 alpha, 16px blur, 8px down).
  - Never clipped, strictly zero hard box edges.
- **Satirical Pop-Art & Meme Metaphors**:
  - Parody branding: e.g. Marlboro cigarette pack labeled `"Adobe"` filled with creative software pens (`Id`, `Ai`, `Ps`, `Mu`, `An`).
  - Anatomical brain with halftone dot-screen texture and torn deckle paper edges.
  - Stick figure carrying an anatomically detailed brain saying `"PLEASE USE IT"` inside a die-cut speech bubble.
  - Floating Spotify-character with headphones wired down into a levitating phone against a brand logo circle.
- **3D Kinetic Lettering Badges**:
  - Bold comic emphasis words (e.g. `"GOOD!"`, `"CRASH!"`, `"WIN!"`) rendered in extruded 3D lettering with yellow face, red 3D depth extrusion, and thick white outer die-cut border.

### 3.1 Master Prompt Formula for Google Flow (Sticker Style)
```text
An isolated pop-art die-cut vinyl sticker of [SUBJECT, e.g. a retro satirical cigarette pack parody with bold 'Adobe' branding and colorful software pens inside], thick crisp solid white contour outline die-cut border, subtle drop shadow, glossy vinyl texture, vibrant saturated pop-art colors, solid #FF00FF magenta background for lossless chroma keying, clean vector edges, highly detailed sticker illustration.
```

# 4. 🎬 THE EDITORIAL ANIMATION LAW (How They Actually Move)

| Style | Entrance Animation | Camera Movement | Micro-Emphasis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stickman** | Quick 0.20s spring pop with gentle settle (`scale: 0.85 -> 1.02 -> 1.0`). Limbs stay static, no uncanny morphing. | Subtle 3% drift zoom on key realization. | 0.12s haptic punch when hitting a dramatic obstacle or surprise. |
| **Vox Explainer** | Smooth 0.28s rise-and-fade (`y: +40px -> 0`, `opacity: 0 -> 1`), highlighter sweeps left-to-right (`clip-path` wipe in 0.35s). | Cinematic smooth whip pan (0.35s cubic ease) between split comparisons, slow slow zoom (2.5%). | Dotted trajectory lines draw organically; Polaroid pins land with a single crisp click. |
| **Sticker Pop** | Vibrant 0.18s spring bounce (`scale: 0.70 -> 1.08 -> 1.0`, `rotation: -3deg -> 0deg`). | Punch-in zoom (4.5%) on keyword hits. | Single 3px settle pulse on landing, strictly ZERO continuous jitter loops. |
